# Code of Conduct · 行为准则

> MISTVERSE is a hobby project. We follow the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) spirit. This document is bilingual: **English first**, then 中文.

---

## 1. Our Pledge · 承诺

### English

We pledge to make participation in MISTVERSE a welcoming and inclusive experience for everyone — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

### 中文

本项目欢迎所有参与者，承诺营造一个无骚扰的协作环境，无论年龄、体型、残疾、族裔、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向。

---

## 2. Expected behavior · 期望行为

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

---

## 3. Unacceptable behavior · 不可接受的行为

- Sexist, racist, homophobic, transphobic, ableist, or otherwise discriminatory language or actions.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission ("doxxing").
- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal/political attacks.
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## 4. Enforcement · 执行

### English

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

### 中文

项目维护者有责任澄清可接受行为的标准，并会对任何不可接受的行为做出适当且公平的纠正。

维护者有权删除、编辑或拒绝与本行为准则不符的评论、commit、代码、wiki 编辑、Issue 及其他贡献。

---

## 5. Reporting · 报告

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, **report it privately** through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/badhope/mistverse/security/advisories/new) (the maintainer will be notified directly). All reports will be handled with discretion.

如遇违反行为，请通过 [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/badhope/mistverse/security/advisories/new) 私下联系维护者。所有报告都会被妥善处理。
